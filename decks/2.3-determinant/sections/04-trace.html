The trace

The sum of the diagonal.

Act 4 / Trace

Definition and rules

$$\operatorname{tr}A = \sum_i a_{ii}$$

Entry \(a_{ii}\) is how much axis \(i\) stretches along itself. The trace adds those up: the total stretch.

Sums

\(\operatorname{tr}(A+B) = \operatorname{tr}A + \operatorname{tr}B\), since \((A+B)_{ii} = a_{ii} + b_{ii}\).

Cyclic

\(\operatorname{tr}(AB) = \sum_i\sum_k a_{ik}\,b_{ki} = \operatorname{tr}(BA)\), even when \(AB \ne BA\).

Preview, Block 2.4: trace = sum of eigenvalues, determinant = product of eigenvalues.

Act 4 / Preview

One example

\(A = \begin{bmatrix}3&1\\2&2\end{bmatrix}\) has \(\operatorname{tr}A = 5\) and \(\det A = 4\).

Its eigenvalues turn out to be 1 and 4: sum \(1 + 4 = 5\), product \(1\cdot 4 = 4\).
