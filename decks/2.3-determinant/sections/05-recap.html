Act 5 / Recap

Everything in this block

Determinant

Signed area or volume factor. \(ad - bc\) in 2×2.

Inverse

Exists iff \(\det A \neq 0\). Use solve, not inv.

Rank

Dimension of the column space. \(n = \text{rank} + \text{nullity}\).

Trace

Sum of the diagonal. \(\operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(BA)\).

Invertible, full rank and \(\det \neq 0\) are one condition.

Act 5 / Practice

By hand

  1. Is \(\begin{bmatrix}2&4\\1&2\end{bmatrix}\) invertible? \(\det = 4 - 4 = 0\), so no. Rank 1.
  2. Find \(A^{-1}\) for \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}\), then check \(AA^{-1} = I\).
  3. \(\det(A) = 3\) and \(\det(B) = -2\) for 4×4 matrices. Find \(\det(AB)\), \(\det(A^{-1})\) and \(\det(2A)\).
  4. Show \(\operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(BA)\) for two 2×2 matrices of your choice.
