Block 2.3 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Read the determinant as a signed area or volume factor, and compute it for 2×2 and 3×3
  2. Decide with the determinant whether a matrix has an inverse, and find it for 2×2
  3. Solve \(A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}\) without forming \(A^{-1}\)
  4. Find the rank, and say what rank–nullity tells you
  5. Compute a trace and use its two basic rules
