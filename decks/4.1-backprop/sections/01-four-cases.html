Act 1 / With respect to a matrix

A scalar loss, a matrix of weights

$$L: \mathbb R^{m \times n} \to \mathbb R \qquad \frac{\partial L}{\partial W} = \begin{bmatrix} \partial L/\partial w_{11} & \cdots & \partial L/\partial w_{1n} \\ \vdots & & \vdots \\ \partial L/\partial w_{m1} & \cdots & \partial L/\partial w_{mn} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb R^{m \times n}$$

The gradient has the same shape as the weights, so the update \(W \leftarrow W - \eta\,\partial L/\partial W\) type-checks.
