Act 5 / Recap

Everything in this block

Shapes

One entry per (output, input) pair. \(\partial L/\partial W\) has the shape of \(W\).

Identities

\(\nabla \mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf x = \mathbf a\), \(\nabla \mathbf x^{\top}A\mathbf x = (A + A^{\top})\mathbf x\), \(\partial L/\partial W = \boldsymbol\delta\mathbf x^{\top}\).

Backprop

Chain rule from the loss backwards, reusing each layer's \(\boldsymbol\delta\).

Autodiff

Record the forward pass, sweep it in reverse. One sweep, every gradient.
