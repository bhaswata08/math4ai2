Act 6 / Recap

Everything in this block

Shapes

One entry per (output, input) pair. \(\nabla_{W} L\) has the shape of \(W\).

Identities

\(\nabla \mathbf a^{\top}\mathbf x = \mathbf a\), \(\nabla \mathbf x^{\top}A\mathbf x = (A + A^{\top})\mathbf x\).

The model

\(\mathbf a_i = \mathbf b_i + W_i\mathbf h_{i-1}\), \(\mathbf h_i = g(\mathbf a_i)\). Softmax output, cross entropy, so \(L(\theta) = -\log\hat y_\ell\).

The seed

\(\nabla_{\mathbf a_L} L = -(\mathbf e_\ell - \hat{\mathbf y})\). Prediction minus truth, and that cancellation is why the pair is standard.

The sweep

\(\nabla_{\mathbf h_{k-1}} L = W_k^{\top}\nabla_{\mathbf a_k} L\), then \(\odot\, g'(\mathbf a_{k-1})\). Parameters: \(\nabla_{W_k} L = \nabla_{\mathbf a_k} L\,\mathbf h_{k-1}^{\top}\), \(\nabla_{\mathbf b_k} L = \nabla_{\mathbf a_k} L\).

Autodiff

Record the forward pass, sweep it in reverse. One sweep, every gradient, and nobody writes the formulas down.

  5. Take \(g = \tanh\) and a layer with \(\mathbf a_k = (0, 3)^{\top}\). Compute \(g'(\mathbf a_k)\) and say which of the two units will pass almost nothing down.
