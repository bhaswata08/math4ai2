Block 4.1 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Say the shape of any derivative before computing it
  2. Differentiate linear and quadratic forms with the standard identities
  3. Write down a feedforward network and say why softmax pairs with cross entropy
  4. Derive all five backpropagation equations, from the output seed to the weight gradients
  5. Explain what reverse-mode automatic differentiation computes, and why it is cheap
Block 4.1 / Notation

The symbols, once, so they are not a surprise later

\(\mathbf x \in \mathbb R^n\)the input; also \(\mathbf h_0\)
\(L\)layer count: \(L-1\) hidden, plus output
\(\mathbf a_i\)pre-activation: \(\mathbf b_i + W_i\mathbf h_{i-1}\)
\(\mathbf h_i\)activation: \(g(\mathbf a_i)\)
\(g\)the nonlinearity, entry by entry
\(O\)output function: linear or softmax
\(\hat{\mathbf y} = \mathbf h_L\)the prediction, \(k\)-dimensional
\(\ell\)index of the true class
\(\theta\)\([W_1,\dots,W_L,\mathbf b_1,\dots,\mathbf b_L]\)
\(L(\theta)\)the loss; for classification \(-\log \hat y_\ell\)

Two warnings. \(L\) is both the layer count and the loss, as in Khapra's lectures; the loss always carries its \((\theta)\). And the 3Blue1Brown clips label things \(C\), \(a\), \(z\), \(\sigma\): that is \(L(\theta)\), \(\mathbf h\), \(\mathbf a\), \(g\) here.
