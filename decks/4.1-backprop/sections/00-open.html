Block 4.1 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Say the shape of any derivative before computing it
  2. Differentiate linear and quadratic forms with the standard identities
  3. Derive the backpropagation equations for a small network with the chain rule
  4. Explain what reverse-mode automatic differentiation computes, and why it is cheap
