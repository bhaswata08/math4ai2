Act 4 / 4.4
  • Start with the easy case: a deep network that is only one neuron wide.
  • Every quantity is then a single number, and the chain rule gives the derivative directly.
$$\frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial W_{111}} = \frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial \hat y}\,\frac{\partial \hat y}{\partial a_{L11}}\,\frac{\partial a_{L11}}{\partial h_{21}}\,\frac{\partial h_{21}}{\partial a_{21}}\,\frac{\partial a_{21}}{\partial h_{11}}\,\frac{\partial h_{11}}{\partial a_{11}}\,\frac{\partial a_{11}}{\partial W_{111}}$$ $$\frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial W_{111}} = \frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial h_{11}}\,\frac{\partial h_{11}}{\partial W_{111}} \qquad \text{(just compressing the chain rule)}$$ $$\frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial W_{211}} = \frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial h_{21}}\,\frac{\partial h_{21}}{\partial W_{211}}$$ $$\frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial W_{L11}} = \frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial a_{L1}}\,\frac{\partial a_{L1}}{\partial W_{L11}}$$
Act 4 / 4.4
  • Put the question to \(W_L\), \(b_L\) and \(h_L\): what went wrong here?
  • \(W_L\) and \(b_L\) own their share. \(h_L\) does not, and says it is only ever as good as the pre-activation layer feeding it.
  • That layer answers the same way, pointing at the hidden layer and the weights below it.
  • Carry on down and the responsibility turns out to be spread across every weight and bias in the model.
  • You cannot reach a deep weight directly. You reach it through the hidden layers and the output layer, and the chain rule is exactly what permits that.
$$\underbrace{\frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial W_{111}}}_{\substack{\text{Talk to the}\\\text{weight directly}}} = \underbrace{\frac{\partial \mathscr L(\theta)}{\partial \hat y}\,\frac{\partial \hat y}{\partial a_3}}_{\substack{\text{Talk to the}\\\text{output layer}}}\;\underbrace{\frac{\partial a_3}{\partial h_2}\,\frac{\partial h_2}{\partial a_2}}_{\substack{\text{Talk to the}\\\text{previous hidden}\\\text{layer}}}\;\underbrace{\frac{\partial a_2}{\partial h_1}\,\frac{\partial h_1}{\partial a_1}}_{\substack{\text{Talk to the}\\\text{previous}\\\text{hidden layer}}}\;\underbrace{\frac{\partial a_1}{\partial W_{111}}}_{\substack{\text{and now}\\\text{talk to the}\\\text{weights}}}$$
