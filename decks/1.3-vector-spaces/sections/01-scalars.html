A coordinate pair is two scalars

$$\begin{bmatrix} \textcolor{#8bd17c}{3} \\ \textcolor{#e8798a}{-2} \end{bmatrix} = \textcolor{#8bd17c}{3}\,\hat\imath + \textcolor{#e8798a}{(-2)}\,\hat\jmath$$

Every vector is built by scaling, then adding. Next: do the same with any two arrows you like.
