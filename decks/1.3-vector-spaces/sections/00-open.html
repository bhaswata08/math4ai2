Block 1.3 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Read a coordinate pair as scalars on \(\hat\imath\) and \(\hat\jmath\)
  2. Build a linear combination of vectors, and compute it
  3. Say what the span of some vectors is: a plane, a line or a point
  4. Decide whether vectors are linearly independent
  5. Say what a vector space and a subspace are, and list every subspace of \(\mathbb{R}^2\) and \(\mathbb{R}^3\)
