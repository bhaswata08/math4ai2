Act 6 / Recap

Everything in this block

Linear combination

Scale, then add: \(a\vec v + b\vec w\). A coordinate pair is one with \(\hat\imath, \hat\jmath\).

Span

All combinations: a plane, a line through 0, or just 0.

Independence

No vector is a combination of the others. Only the zero mix gives \(\vec 0\).

Vector space

Closed under add and scale, contains \(\vec 0\). A subspace sits inside another.

Next block: basis and dimension, the smallest independent set that spans.

By hand

  1. \(2\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} - 3\begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\) \(= \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 4 - 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\)
  2. Write \(\begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\) as a combination of \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\) and \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\).
  3. What is the span of \(\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -4 \end{bmatrix}\) and \(\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}\): the plane, a line or a point?
  4. Are \(\left\{\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 0 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \\ 7 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 9 \\ 11 \end{bmatrix}\right\}\) linearly independent?
  5. Is \(\{[x, y] : y = x + 1\}\) a subspace of \(\mathbb{R}^2\)? Say which rule decides it.
