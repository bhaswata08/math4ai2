Add a third vector

On the plane, the span does not change. Off the plane, it grows to all of 3D.

Redundant means a combination of the others

If \(\vec u = a\vec v + b\vec w\), then \(\vec u\) adds nothing: the vectors are dependent.

Act 4 / The test

Only the zero mix gives zero

$$a\,\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} + b\,\textcolor{#c792ea}{\vec w} + c\,\textcolor{#f0a35e}{\vec u} = \vec 0$$

Dependent

Some choice with not all of \(a, b, c\) zero gives \(\vec 0\).

\(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}\) and \(\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}\): \(\;2\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} - 1\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix} = \vec 0\)

Independent

Only \(a = b = c = 0\) gives \(\vec 0\).

\(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\) and \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\): \(\;a + b = 0\) and \(b = 0\), so \(a = 0\)
Act 4 / By elimination

Three vectors: the test is three equations

Take \(\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} = [1, 2, 1]\), \(\textcolor{#c792ea}{\vec w} = [2, 1, 0]\), \(\textcolor{#f0a35e}{\vec u} = [3, 3, 1]\). Write \(a\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} + b\textcolor{#c792ea}{\vec w} + c\textcolor{#f0a35e}{\vec u} = \vec 0\) one entry at a time:

$$\begin{aligned} a + 2b + 3c &= 0 \\ 2a + b + 3c &= 0 \\ a \phantom{{}+2b} + c &= 0 \end{aligned} \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \begin{bmatrix} \textcolor{#6fd0c0}{1} & \textcolor{#c792ea}{2} & \textcolor{#f0a35e}{3} \\ \textcolor{#6fd0c0}{2} & \textcolor{#c792ea}{1} & \textcolor{#f0a35e}{3} \\ \textcolor{#6fd0c0}{1} & \textcolor{#c792ea}{0} & \textcolor{#f0a35e}{1} \end{bmatrix}$$

The vectors become the columns. Three row operations never change the solutions, because each one can be undone: swap two rows, multiply a row by a nonzero number, add a multiple of one row to another.

Act 4 / By elimination

Row echelon form, then RREF

$$\begin{bmatrix} 1&2&3\\2&1&3\\1&0&1 \end{bmatrix} \xrightarrow{\;R_2-2R_1,\;R_3-R_1 \;} \begin{bmatrix} 1&2&3\\0&-3&-3\\0&-2&-2 \end{bmatrix} \xrightarrow{\;-\frac13 R_2,\;R_3+2R_2 \;} \begin{bmatrix} 1&2&3\\0&1&1\\0&0&0 \end{bmatrix}$$

Row echelon form: each row's first nonzero entry, its pivot, sits right of the one above.

$$\xrightarrow{\;R_1-2R_2 \;}\begin{bmatrix} 1&0&1\\0&1&1\\0&0&0 \end{bmatrix}$$

RREF: also every pivot is 1 and alone in its column.

Act 4 / By elimination

Read the answer off the RREF

The two nonzero rows say \(a + c = 0\) and \(b + c = 0\). Column 3 has no pivot, so \(c\) is free: any value works.

\(a = -c\) and \(b = -c\). Take \(c = -1\): then \(a = 1\), \(b = 1\), and

$$1\,\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} + 1\,\textcolor{#c792ea}{\vec w} - 1\,\textcolor{#f0a35e}{\vec u} = \vec 0 \quad\text{so}\quad \textcolor{#f0a35e}{\vec u} = \textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} + \textcolor{#c792ea}{\vec w}$$

Pivot in every column

Only \(a = b = c = 0\) works. Independent.

A column without a pivot

A free variable, so a nonzero mix gives \(\vec 0\). Dependent, and the RREF gives the constants.
