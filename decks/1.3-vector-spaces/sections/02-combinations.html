Act 2 / Definition

A linear combination

$$\textcolor{#f0a35e}{a}\,\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} + \textcolor{#f0a35e}{b}\,\textcolor{#c792ea}{\vec w}$$

Scale each vector by a number, then add. The numbers \(a\) and \(b\) are scalars.

$$2\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}} + (-1)\textcolor{#c792ea}{\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 - 3 \\ 4 + 1 \end{bmatrix} = \textcolor{#f0a35e}{\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}}$$

Act 2 / Where this shows up

Attention output is a linear combination

A transformer mixes the value vectors of two words with attention weights.

$$0.7\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}} + 0.3\textcolor{#c792ea}{\begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}} = \textcolor{#f0a35e}{\begin{bmatrix} 1.4 \\ 1.2 \end{bmatrix}}$$

The result lies between the two inputs.
