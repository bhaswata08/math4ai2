Act 5 / Recap

Everything in this block

Taylor

Match derivatives at a point: \(c_n = f^{(n)}(a)/n!\).

Hessian

\(f(\mathbf p + \mathbf h) \approx f(\mathbf p) + \nabla f^{\top}\mathbf h + \tfrac12\mathbf h^{\top}H\mathbf h\).

Critical points

\(\nabla f = 0\). Signs of \(H\)'s eigenvalues: min, max or saddle.

Convexity

Chords above the graph, \(H \succeq 0\). Local minimum means global minimum.
