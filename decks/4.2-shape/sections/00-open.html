Block 4.2 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Build the Taylor polynomial of a function and say what each term controls
  2. Write the quadratic approximation of \(f(\mathbf x)\) with the gradient and the Hessian
  3. Classify a critical point as a minimum, maximum or saddle from the Hessian's eigenvalues
  4. Test a function for convexity, and say why convexity makes optimisation easy
