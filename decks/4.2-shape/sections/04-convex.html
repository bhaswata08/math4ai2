The chord test

Convex: every chord lies on or above the graph. One chord below is enough to fail.

The test, direction by direction

\(f = x^2 - y^2\): the chord is above along \(x\) but below along \(y\), and \(H = \mathrm{diag}(2,-2)\) is not positive semi-definite. \(f = x^2 + y^2\) passes in every direction.

Act 4 / Tests and examples

How to recognise one

Test

Twice differentiable \(f\) is convex exactly when \(H(\mathbf x)\) is positive semi-definite everywhere.

Payoff

Every local minimum is a global minimum, so gradient descent cannot get trapped.
