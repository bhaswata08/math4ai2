Projection

The shadow one vector casts on the line of another.

20 min

Drop w onto the line through v

The shadow is \(\operatorname{proj}_{\vec v} \vec w = \dfrac{\vec v \cdot \vec w}{\vec v \cdot \vec v}\,\vec v\). It is a multiple of \(\vec v\).

Act 4 / Example

Project [1, 2] onto [3, 1]

$$\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v \cdot \vec v} = 10, \quad \textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} \cdot \textcolor{#c792ea}{\vec w} = 3 + 2 = 5, \quad \operatorname{proj} = \frac{5}{10}\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \textcolor{#f0a35e}{\begin{bmatrix} 1.5 \\ 0.5 \end{bmatrix}}$$

Leftover: \(\vec w - \operatorname{proj} = [-0.5, 1.5]\).

Check: \([3, 1] \cdot [-0.5, 1.5] = -1.5 + 1.5 = 0\), so it is perpendicular to \(\vec v\).
