Projection

The shadow one vector casts on the line of another.

20 min

Drop w onto the line through v

The shadow is \(\hat v\,(\hat v \cdot \vec w)\): the unit direction \(\hat v = \vec v / \lVert \vec v \rVert\), times the shadow length \(\hat v \cdot \vec w\).

Act 4 / Example

Project [1, 2] onto [3, 1]

$$\operatorname{proj} = \hat v\,(\hat v \cdot \textcolor{#c792ea}{\vec w}), \qquad \hat v = \frac{\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v}}{\lVert \textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} \rVert}$$

  1. Unit direction: \(\lVert \vec v \rVert = \sqrt{3^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{10}\), so \(\hat v = \frac{1}{\sqrt{10}}[3, 1]\).
  2. Shadow length: \(\hat v \cdot \vec w = \frac{1}{\sqrt{10}}(3 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot 2) = \frac{5}{\sqrt{10}}\).
  3. Shadow: \(\operatorname{proj} = \frac{5}{\sqrt{10}} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{10}}\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \frac{5}{10}\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \textcolor{#f0a35e}{\begin{bmatrix} 1.5 \\ 0.5 \end{bmatrix}}\).

Leftover: \(\vec w - \operatorname{proj} = [-0.5, 1.5]\).

Check: \([3, 1] \cdot [-0.5, 1.5] = -1.5 + 1.5 = 0\), so it is perpendicular to \(\vec v\).
