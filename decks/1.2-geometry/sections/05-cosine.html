Cosine similarity

Compare direction, ignore size.

20 min

The dot product holds the angle

\(\vec v \cdot \vec w = \lVert \vec v \rVert \lVert \vec w \rVert \cos\theta\), so \(\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec v \cdot \vec w}{\lVert \vec v \rVert \lVert \vec w \rVert}\). It runs from 1 to \(-1\).

Stretching a vector changes nothing

The dot product triples when \(\vec w\) becomes \(3\vec w\). The cosine does not move.

Act 5 / Example

The angle between [3, 1] and [1, 2]

$$\cos\theta = \frac{5}{\sqrt{10}\,\sqrt{5}} = \frac{5}{\sqrt{50}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \approx \textcolor{#f0a35e}{0.71}, \qquad \theta = \textcolor{#ffd166}{45^\circ}$$

Triple the second vector to \([3, 6]\): the dot product is 15, the length is \(3\sqrt{5}\).

\(\cos\theta = \dfrac{15}{3\sqrt{50}} = 0.71\), unchanged.
