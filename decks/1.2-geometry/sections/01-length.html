Length from two legs

The x part and the y part are the legs of a right triangle. \(\lVert \vec v \rVert = \sqrt{3^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{13}\).

Act 1 / Any dimension

Same rule for n entries

$$\lVert \textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} \rVert_2 = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 + \cdots + v_n^2} = \sqrt{\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} \cdot \textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v}}$$

For \(\vec v = [1, 2, 2]\): \(\sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = \sqrt{9} = \textcolor{#f0a35e}{3}\).

The length is the square root of the vector's dot product with itself.
