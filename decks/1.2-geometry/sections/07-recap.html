Act 7 / Recap

Everything in this block

Norms

L2 is the length, L1 walks the grid, L∞ is the biggest entry.

Unit vector

\(\hat v = \vec v / \lVert \vec v \rVert\) keeps direction, length 1.

Projection

\(\dfrac{\vec v \cdot \vec w}{\vec v \cdot \vec v}\,\vec v\), the shadow of \(\vec w\) on \(\vec v\).

Cosine

\(\cos\theta\) compares direction only, from \(-1\) to 1.

Orthogonal

Dot product zero. Orthonormal adds length 1.
