Unit vectors

Keep the direction, throw the length away.

10 min

Divide by your own length

Each vector lands on the unit circle, still pointing the same way. \(\hat v = \vec v / \lVert \vec v \rVert\).

Act 3 / Example

Normalizing [3, 4]

$$\textcolor{#6fd0c0}{\vec v} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \lVert \vec v \rVert = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5, \quad \textcolor{#f0a35e}{\hat v} = \frac{1}{5}\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix} = \textcolor{#f0a35e}{\begin{bmatrix} 0.6 \\ 0.8 \end{bmatrix}}$$

Check: \(0.6^2 + 0.8^2 = 0.36 + 0.64 = 1\).

The zero vector has no direction, so it cannot be normalized.
