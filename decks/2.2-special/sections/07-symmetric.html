Think of a road-distance chart between cities: the distance from city \(i\) to city \(j\) equals the distance from \(j\) to \(i\), so the chart reads the same across the diagonal. Only square matrices can do that. Covariance, Hessian and Gram matrices are this same idea:

Covariance

\(\mathrm{Cov}_{ij}=\mathrm{Cov}_{ji}\)

Hessian

Mixed second derivatives agree: \(\partial_i\partial_j f=\partial_j\partial_i f\)

Gram matrix

\(X^TX\), because \((X^TX)^T=X^TX\)
