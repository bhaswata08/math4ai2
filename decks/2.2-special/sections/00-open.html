Block 2.2 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Name the map that does nothing, \(I\), and the map that squashes everything, the zero matrix
  2. Read a diagonal matrix as a scaling of each axis, and a negative entry as a reflection
  3. Recognise a shear, and say why it keeps area
  4. Test whether a matrix is orthogonal, and say what it preserves
  5. Recognise a projection as squashing onto a line, and explain why it cannot be undone
  6. Say what an inverse is, and undo a scaling, a shear or a rotation
  7. Translate coordinates between two bases with a matrix and its inverse, and write a transformation in another basis as \(B^{-1}MB\)
  8. Spot a symmetric matrix, read \(x^TAx\) as a surface (bowl, trough or saddle), test a symmetric \(2\times2\) for positive definite, and say why a positive definite Hessian means a minimum
