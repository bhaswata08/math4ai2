The identity

The map that does nothing.

Nothing moves

\(I = \begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\) sends every point to itself: the marked point, the F and the axes stay put.

Act 1 / Definition

The identity matrix

$$I\,\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}, \qquad AI = IA = A$$

\(I_n\) has 1 on the diagonal and 0 elsewhere. Its columns are the standard basis vectors.

\(I\) plays the role of the number 1 for matrices.
