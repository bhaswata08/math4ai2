The family at a glance

MatrixActionKey fact
Identitydo nothing\(AI=IA=A\)
Diagonalscale each axis; negative flipsarea \(\times\) product of entries
Shearslantarea kept
Orthogonalrotate or reflect\(Q^{-1}=Q^T\), lengths kept
Projectiondrop onto a line\(P=uu^T\), \(P^2=P\), no inverse
Inverseundo\(A^{-1}A=I\)
Symmetricmirror across diagonal\(A=A^T\)

Each one has a single-word action. Block 2.3 measures that action with one number, the determinant.

Act 9 / Practice

By hand

  1. Is \(Q=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}\) orthogonal? Find \(Q^{-1}\). Columns \((0,1)\) and \((1,0)\) are unit and perpendicular, so \(Q^TQ=I\) and \(Q^{-1}=Q^T=Q\). It is a reflection across the line \(y=x\).
  2. Write \(\mathrm{diag}(-2,\,0.5)\) as a reflection times two one-axis scalings.
  3. Build \(P=uu^T\) for \(u=\tfrac15\begin{bmatrix}3\\4\end{bmatrix}\) and check \(P^2=P\).
  4. Is \(\begin{bmatrix}2&3\\3&4\end{bmatrix}\) positive definite?
