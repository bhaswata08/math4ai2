Block 2.2 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Name the map that does nothing, and use it to state what an inverse is
  2. Read the determinant as a signed area or volume factor, and compute it for 2×2 and 3×3
  3. Decide whether a matrix has an inverse, and find it for 2×2
  4. Solve \(A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}\) without forming \(A^{-1}\)
  5. Find the rank, and say what rank–nullity tells you
  6. Compute a trace and use its two basic rules
