Block 1.4 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Say what a basis is, and test whether a set of vectors is one
  2. Say what dimension means, and give it for a line, a plane and a space
  3. Find the coordinates of a vector in a basis other than the standard one
  4. Translate coordinates between two bases by hand
  5. Find the intersection of two subspaces
