Act 5 / Recap

Everything in this block

Stretch

\(f'(x)\) is how much \(f\) stretches the line near \(x\). Negative means a flip.

Gradient

\(\nabla f\): all partials in one vector. Points uphill, length is steepness.

Directional

\(D_{\mathbf u} f = \nabla f \cdot \mathbf u\). Steepest along \(\nabla f\), zero along contours.

Jacobian

\(J\): the matrix a map looks like up close. \(\det J\) scales area.

Tomorrow: chain Jacobians together, and you have backpropagation.
