Block 3.4 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Read a derivative as how much a map stretches space near a point
  2. Compute a gradient and say what its direction and length mean
  3. Compute a directional derivative and find the steepest direction
  4. Build the Jacobian of a vector-valued function and read it as a local linear map
