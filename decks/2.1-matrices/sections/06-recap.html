Act 6 / Recap

Everything in this block

Matrix

\(m \times n\) table; transpose swaps rows and columns.

Linear map

\(A\mathbf{v}\) mixes the columns; the grid stays straight and the origin fixed.

Product

\(AB\): \(B\) first, then \(A\). Not commutative.

Hadamard, affine

\(\odot\) is entrywise. \(A\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}\) moves the origin.
