Matrix, then shift

With \(\mathbf{y} = A\mathbf{x}\) the origin stays. With \(+\,\mathbf{b}\) it moves to \(\mathbf{b}\).

Act 5 / Definition

Affine is not linear

$$\mathbf{y} = A\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b} \qquad A\mathbf{0} + \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{b} \neq \mathbf{0}$$

A linear map must send \(\mathbf{0}\) to \(\mathbf{0}\). With \(\mathbf{b} \neq \mathbf{0}\) this one does not.

A dense layer is affine, then a nonlinearity: \(\sigma(W\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b})\).

Act 5 / One matrix

Homogeneous coordinates

$$\begin{bmatrix} A & \mathbf{b} \\ \mathbf{0}^\top & 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{x} \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} A\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b} \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

Append a 1 to the input. The shift becomes part of one bigger matrix.
