Block 2.1 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Read a matrix: its shape, its entries, its transpose
  2. Compute \(A\mathbf{v}\) as a mix of the columns of \(A\), and say what a linear map does to the plane
  3. Multiply matrices, and read \(AB\) as "\(B\) first, then \(A\)"
  4. Tell the Hadamard product from the matrix product
  5. Tell an affine map \(A\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}\) from a linear one, and write it as one matrix
