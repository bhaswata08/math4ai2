Entry times entry

\((A \odot B)_{ij} = a_{ij}\,b_{ij}\). Both matrices have the same shape. A 0-1 matrix works as a mask.

Act 4 / Compare

Not a composition

\(A \odot B\) does not mean "one map after another". It has no picture as moving the plane.

It is commutative: \(A \odot B = B \odot A\). The matrix product is not.

In AI: dropout masks, attention masks, and the gates in LSTM and GRU cells.
