The Hadamard product

Multiply matching entries. A different operation with the same look.

Entry times entry

\((A \odot B)_{ij} = a_{ij}\,b_{ij}\). Both matrices have the same shape. A matrix of 0s and 1s works as a mask: a 1 keeps the entry, a 0 sets it to zero.

Act 4 / Compare

Not a composition

\(A \odot B\) does not mean "one map after another". It has no picture as moving the plane.

It is commutative: \(A \odot B = B \odot A\). The matrix product is not.

In AI: dropout masks, attention masks, and the gates in LSTM and GRU cells.

Mask a row

A mask is a row of 0s and 1s. Under \(\odot\), a 1 keeps the entry and a 0 zeroes it.

  1. \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \odot \begin{bmatrix} 7 & 8 & 9 \end{bmatrix}\) \(= \begin{bmatrix} 7 & 0 & 9 \end{bmatrix}\)
  2. Find the mask \(\mathbf m\) with \(\mathbf m \odot \begin{bmatrix} 7 & 8 & 9 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 8 & 0 \end{bmatrix}\).
