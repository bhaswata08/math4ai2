Block 1.1 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Read a vector three ways: a list, an arrow, a data point
  2. Move between coordinates and arrows in both directions
  3. Add vectors and scale them, by picture and by arithmetic
  4. Compute a dot product and say what its sign means
