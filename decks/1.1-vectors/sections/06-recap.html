Act 6 / Recap

Everything in this block

A vector

A list of numbers, an arrow from the origin, a data point. Coordinates translate between them.

Add

Tip to tail, or entry by entry: \(\begin{bmatrix} v_1 + w_1 \\ v_2 + w_2 \end{bmatrix}\)

Scale

Stretch, squish or flip: \(c\,\vec v = \begin{bmatrix} c v_1 \\ c v_2 \end{bmatrix}\)

Dot product

\(\sum_i v_i w_i\), or projection times length. Its sign is an angle test.

Next block: length, projection and cosine similarity, all built from the dot product.

Act 6 / Practice

By hand

  1. \(\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}\) \(= 2 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot 3 = 5\)
  2. \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}\). What does the answer say about the angle?
  3. Without drawing: do \(\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\) and \(\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}\) point roughly the same way or apart?
  4. \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 & -1 \end{bmatrix}^{T} \cdot \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 5 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}^{T}\)
  5. \(2\left(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}\right) \cdot \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}\)
