Act 6 / Recap

Everything in this block

Update

\(\mathbf w \leftarrow \mathbf w - \eta\,\nabla L\). Steps shrink as the slope does.

Learning rate

Stable below \(2/\lambda_{\max}\). Too small crawls, too large diverges.

Valleys

Unequal curvature makes the path zig-zag. The condition number measures how badly.

Batches

Average the gradient over a sample. Mini-batches trade a little noise for far more updates.
