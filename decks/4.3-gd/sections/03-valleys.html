Narrow valleys

When the bowl is stretched, the steepest direction is not the way to the bottom.

Zig-zag

\(L = \tfrac12 w_1^2 + 4w_2^2\). Along \(w_1\) each step multiplies by \(1-\eta\), across \(w_2\) by \(1-8\eta\). Small \(\eta\) crawls, \(0.22\) zig-zags, \(0.27\) blows up across the valley.

Act 3 / What the Hessian says

One step size, two curvatures

$$H = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 8 \end{bmatrix} \qquad \kappa = \frac{\lambda_{\max}}{\lambda_{\min}} = 8$$

\(\eta\) must stay below \(2/\lambda_{\max} = 2/8 = 0.25\) to avoid blowing up across the valley, which makes progress along it slow. At \(\eta = 0.22\) the across-factor is \(-0.76\): still shrinking, but with a visible wobble. The larger \(\kappa\), the worse the zig-zag.

Momentum: let the zig-zags cancel

\(v\) is the velocity, a running sum of past steps. \(\beta\) is the friction: the share of \(v\) kept each step (\(\beta = 0.3\) here).

$$v \leftarrow \beta\,v - \eta\,\nabla L(\mathbf w)$$ $$\mathbf w \leftarrow \mathbf w + v$$

Across the valley the kicks alternate in sign, so they cancel in \(v\). Along it they point the same way, so they add up.

Same \(\eta = 0.22\): plain GD needs 15 steps, momentum 8, to reach \(|\mathbf w| < 0.1\).
