Act 3 / What the Hessian says

One step size, two curvatures

$$H = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 8 \end{bmatrix} \qquad \kappa = \frac{\lambda_{\max}}{\lambda_{\min}} = 8$$

\(\eta\) must stay below \(2/8\) to avoid blowing up across the valley, which makes progress along it slow. The larger \(\kappa\), the worse the zig-zag. Momentum and Adam are designed to fix exactly this.
