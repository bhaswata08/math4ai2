Act 4 / The loss is an average

Estimate the gradient from a sample

$$\nabla L(\mathbf w) = \frac1N \sum_{i=1}^{N} \nabla \ell_i(\mathbf w) \;\approx\; \frac1B \sum_{i \in \text{batch}} \nabla \ell_i(\mathbf w)$$

Batch

\(B = N\). The exact gradient, one update per pass over the data.

Mini-batch

\(B\) of 16 to 512. A good estimate, many updates per pass.

Stochastic

\(B = 1\). A noisy estimate, \(N\) updates per pass.

Same start, three paths

Linear regression on 64 points. Smaller batches give noisier steps toward the same minimum.

Per update, or per unit of work

Per update, batch wins. Per data point processed, the cheap noisy steps win.
