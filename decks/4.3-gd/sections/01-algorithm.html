Act 1 / The update

One line of maths

$$\mathbf w_{k+1} = \mathbf w_k - \eta\,\nabla L(\mathbf w_k)$$

Weights

\(\mathbf w\): every parameter of the model, as one vector.

Gradient

\(\nabla L\): from backpropagation, Block 4.1.

Learning rate

\(\eta\): how far to trust the slope. Chosen by you.

In one dimension

The step is \(\eta\) times the slope, so steps shrink by themselves as the ground flattens.
