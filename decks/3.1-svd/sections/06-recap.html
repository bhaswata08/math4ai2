Act 6 / Recap

Everything in this block

Factor

Any \(A = U\Sigma V^T\): rotate, stretch (and change dimension), rotate.

Compute

\(\mathbf{v}_i\) and \(\sigma_i^2\) from \(A^TA\); then \(\mathbf{u}_i = A\mathbf{v}_i/\sigma_i\).

Relate

For symmetric PSD \(S\), the SVD is \(Q\Lambda Q^T\).

Approximate

\(A_k = \sum_{i\le k}\sigma_i\mathbf{u}_i\mathbf{v}_i^T\) is the best rank-\(k\) matrix.

Act 6 / Practice

By hand

  1. Find the SVD of \(\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&-2\end{bmatrix}\). \(A^TA = \mathrm{diag}(9, 4)\), so \(\sigma = 3, 2\) and \(V = I\). \(\mathbf{u}_1 = A\mathbf{e}_1/3 = (1, 0)\), \(\mathbf{u}_2 = A\mathbf{e}_2/2 = (0, -1)\). So \(U = \mathrm{diag}(1, -1)\), \(\Sigma = \mathrm{diag}(3, 2)\).
  2. Find the singular values and the rank of \(\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}\).
  3. A symmetric matrix has eigenvalues \(4\) and \(-1\). What are its singular values?
  4. A grayscale image is \(1000 \times 800\). How many numbers does a rank-20 approximation store, and what fraction of the original is that?
