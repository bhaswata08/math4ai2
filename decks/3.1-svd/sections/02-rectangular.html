Matrices that change dimension

A rectangular matrix takes vectors from one space to a space of a different size.

15 min

From \(\mathbb{R}^3\) to \(\mathbb{R}^2\)

Which matrix connects a vector with 3 entries to a vector with 2? One with 2 rows and 3 columns.

Three numbers in, two numbers out

\(\begin{bmatrix}3&-2&1\\2&1&-1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\\3\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}2\\1\end{bmatrix}\): one dot product per row.

Act 2 / Shapes

An \(m \times n\) matrix maps \(\mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m\)

  • Columns count the inputs: \(n\).
  • Rows count the outputs: \(m\).
  • A linear layer \(\mathbf{y} = W\mathbf{x}\) with \(W\) of shape \(256 \times 784\) maps a 784-pixel image to 256 features.

Wide matrices lose a dimension. Tall matrices add one.

Flatten a cube

A \(2 \times 3\) matrix pushes the whole cube down onto a plane.

The dimension eraser

\(\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{bmatrix}\) keeps \(x\) and \(y\) and drops \(z\).

Every vector drops onto the plane

\((x, y, z) \mapsto (x, y)\). The height is lost and cannot be recovered.

The dimension adder

\(\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}\) puts a 2D vector into 3D with \(z = 0\).

Erase, then stretch

\(\begin{bmatrix}4&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}4&0&0\\0&2&0\end{bmatrix}\): a rectangular diagonal matrix.

A cylinder, step by step

Erase the height: a disc. Stretch \(x\) by 4 and \(y\) by 2: an ellipse. The combined matrix does both at once.
