Block 3.1 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Describe any matrix as rotate, stretch, rotate
  2. Compute an SVD from the eigenvectors of \(A^TA\)
  3. Relate the SVD to the spectral decomposition \(S = Q\Lambda Q^T\)
  4. Approximate a matrix by keeping its largest singular values
