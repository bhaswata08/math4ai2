Low-rank approximation

Keep the largest singular values, drop the rest, and lose very little.

A sum of rank-1 matrices

Multiply out \(U\Sigma V^T\) column by column: \(A = \sigma_1\mathbf{u}_1\mathbf{v}_1^T + \sigma_2\mathbf{u}_2\mathbf{v}_2^T + \dots\)

Act 5 / Outer products

\(A = \sum_i \sigma_i\,\mathbf{u}_i\mathbf{v}_i^T\)

  • \(\mathbf{u}_i\) (a column) times \(\mathbf{v}_i^T\) (a row) is a full matrix of rank 1.
  • Every row of it is a multiple of \(\mathbf{v}_i^T\).
  • The terms come sorted: \(\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \dots\), so the first ones carry the most.

For \(\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\4&5\end{bmatrix}\): \(\sigma_1\mathbf{u}_1\mathbf{v}_1^T = \tfrac{3\sqrt5}{\sqrt{20}}\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\3&3\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}1.5&1.5\\4.5&4.5\end{bmatrix}\), already close to \(A\).

\(\sigma_2\mathbf{u}_2\mathbf{v}_2^T = \tfrac{\sqrt5}{\sqrt{20}}\begin{bmatrix}3&-3\\-1&1\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}1.5&-1.5\\-0.5&0.5\end{bmatrix}\). The two terms sum to \(\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\4&5\end{bmatrix} = A\).

Layer by layer

Each layer is one column pattern times one row pattern. The running sum rebuilds the matrix.

Act 5 / Truncation

Keep the first \(k\) terms

\(A_k = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \sigma_i\,\mathbf{u}_i\mathbf{v}_i^T\)
  • \(A_k\) has rank \(k\).
  • Storage: \(k\) columns \(\mathbf{u}_i\), \(k\) rows \(\mathbf{v}_i^T\), \(k\) numbers \(\sigma_i\): \(k(m + n + 1)\) instead of \(mn\).

Eckart–Young: no rank-\(k\) matrix is closer to \(A\) than \(A_k\). The error is \(\|A - A_k\|_F^2 = \sigma_{k+1}^2 + \dots + \sigma_r^2\).

An image is a matrix

Rank-\(k\) reconstructions of a grayscale photo. A few dozen terms already look like the original.

Why so few terms are enough

The first 60 of the photo's 240 singular values. They fall off fast: the first 20 hold 96.7% of \(\sum \sigma_i^2\).

Act 5 / In AI

Where low rank shows up

Compressing layers

A \(4096 \times 4096\) weight matrix has 16.8 M entries. Rank 64 needs \(64 \times 8192 \approx 0.5\) M.

LoRA

Fine-tune with a low-rank update \(W + BA\) instead of a full \(\Delta W\).

Recommenders

A users × items rating matrix is close to low rank: a few hidden tastes explain most ratings.

PCA

The SVD of a centred data matrix gives its principal directions (Block 3.2).
