Composition and decomposition

Multiplying many matrices into one is easy. Splitting one matrix back into simple moves is the hard direction.

Which simple moves make up \(M\)?

Given \(M = A_3A_2A_1\), is each factor a scaling, a shear or a rotation? Eigenvectors answer this.

Taking \(M\) apart

\(M\) is a scale, then a reflection, then a shear: the three land on the same F. From \(M\) alone the pieces are hidden. Eigenvectors find them.
