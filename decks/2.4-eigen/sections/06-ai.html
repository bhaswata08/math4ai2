Eigenvectors in AI

The same idea turns up in data, ranking, optimisation and sequence models.

Covariance and its ellipse

  • A cloud of data points, centred: \(X_c\).
  • Its covariance matrix: \(\Sigma = \tfrac1m X_c^TX_c\), which is symmetric.
  • The eigenvectors of \(\Sigma\) are the axes of the cloud's ellipse.
  • Each eigenvalue is the spread of the data along its axis.
  • The longest axis is the first principal component (PCA, Day 3).
