Eigenvectors in AI

The same idea turns up in data, ranking, optimisation and sequence models.

Covariance and its ellipse

The plotted points have covariance \(\Sigma = \tfrac1m X_c^TX_c\), with \(X_c\) the centred data. The eigenvectors of \(\Sigma\) are the ellipse axes; its eigenvalues are the spreads along them.
