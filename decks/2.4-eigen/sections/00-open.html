Block 2.4 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Say what an eigenvector is: a direction the matrix does not turn
  2. Compute eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a matrix by hand
  3. State the spectral theorem for symmetric matrices
  4. Write a matrix as \(PDP^{-1}\) and use it to compute powers
  5. Say where eigenvectors appear in AI
