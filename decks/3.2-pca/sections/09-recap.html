Act 9 / Recap

Everything in this block

Change basis

Centred \(X\), orthonormal \(P\): \(\hat X = XP\), and \(\hat C = P^TCP\) with \(C = \tfrac1m X^TX\).

Three interpretations

Decorrelate, least reconstruction error, most variance. All three give the eigenvectors of \(C\).

Keep \(k\)

Variance along \(\mathbf p_j\) is \(\lambda_j\). Keep the \(k\) largest; the loss is the sum of the rest.

By SVD

\(X = U\Sigma V^T\): \(P = V\), \(\lambda_j = \sigma_j^2/m\), scores \(U\Sigma\).

By hand

For the points \((3, 3), (-3, -3), (1, -1), (-1, 1)\), find \(C\), the principal directions, the 1D scores \(X\mathbf p_1\), and the share of variance kept. \(X^TX = \begin{bmatrix}20&16\\16&20\end{bmatrix}\), so \(C = \begin{bmatrix}5&4\\4&5\end{bmatrix}\), with \(\lambda_1 = 9\), \(\mathbf p_1 = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(1, 1)\) and \(\lambda_2 = 1\), \(\mathbf p_2 = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(1, -1)\). Scores \(X\mathbf p_1 = (3\sqrt2, -3\sqrt2, 0, 0)\). Kept: \(9/10 = 90\%\).
  2. \(C = \begin{bmatrix}5&2\\2&2\end{bmatrix}\). Find \(\lambda_1, \lambda_2\), \(\mathbf p_1\), and the variance kept with \(k = 1\).
  3. Points \((1, 2), (-1, -2), (3, 6), (-3, -6)\). What is \(\lambda_2\), and why?
  4. Why must the data be centred before computing \(C = \tfrac1m X^TX\)?
  5. The SVD of a centred \(X\) (\(m = 50\)) has \(\sigma_1 = 10\), \(\sigma_2 = 5\). What are \(\lambda_1, \lambda_2\), and what share does \(k = 1\) keep?
