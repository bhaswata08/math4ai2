Block 3.2 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Write a dataset in a new orthonormal basis as \(\hat X = XP\) and compute its covariance matrix
  2. Explain PCA three ways: decorrelate, lose the least, keep the most variance
  3. Compute principal components from the eigenvectors of \(C\) or from the SVD of \(X\)
  4. Choose \(k\) from the eigenvalues, and run PCA on a real dataset in NumPy
