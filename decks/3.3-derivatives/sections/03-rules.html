Act 3 / Cheat sheet, part 1

Rules for putting functions together

All of it comes from one definition:

$$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$$
ruleformula
constant\((c)' = 0\)
constant multiple\((c\,f)' = c\,f'\)
sum and difference\((f \pm g)' = f' \pm g'\)
power\((x^n)' = n\,x^{n-1}\)
product\((f g)' = f'g + f g'\)
quotient\(\left(f/g\right)' = \dfrac{f'g - f g'}{g^2}\)
chain\(\big(f(g(x))\big)' = f'(g(x))\cdot g'(x)\)

Act 4 derives the product and chain rules. The quotient rule is the product rule applied to \(f \cdot g^{-1}\).
