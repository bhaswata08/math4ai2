Sums, products, chains

Combine functions, and the nudges combine in a predictable way.

20 min

Sum rule: nudges stack

The change in \(g + h\) is the change in \(g\) plus the change in \(h\).

Product rule: a box gains two strips

\(d(gh) = g\,dh + h\,dg\). The corner \(dg\,dh\) is too small to count.

Chain rule: three number lines

At \(x=1\), a nudge \(dx\) is stretched by \(2\), then by \(0.54\). The stretches multiply: \(1.08\).

Chain rule in general

One local factor per link, multiplied along the chain. Backpropagation is this, repeated.
