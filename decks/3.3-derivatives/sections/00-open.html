Block 3.3 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Think in nudges: a small change in, a proportional change out
  2. Define the derivative as the limit of rise over run, and compute one from it
  3. Derive the power rule, \(1/x\) and \(\sin\) from pictures
  4. Differentiate sums, products and compositions, the last with the chain rule
  5. Take a partial derivative of a function of several inputs
