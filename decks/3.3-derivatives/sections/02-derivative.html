Speed needs two moments

Average speed over \(dt\) is \(ds\) divided by \(dt\).

Shrink the gap

\(dt\) and \(ds\) both go to zero. Their ratio does not.

Secant becomes tangent

The derivative is the slope of the tangent line, and it changes with \(t\).

Act 2 / Definition

The derivative

$$\frac{df}{dx}(x) \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$$

Read it as: output change per unit of input change, for very small changes.

From the definition: \(s(t) = t^3\)

Every term with a leftover \(dt\) vanishes. What survives is \(3t^2\).
