Where does a circle's area come from?

Cut the disc into thin rings of thickness \(dr\).

Unroll one ring

A ring of radius \(r\) is almost a rectangle: \(dA \approx 2\pi r\,dr\).

The error shrinks faster than the nudge

The error is \(\pi\,dr^2\): halve \(dr\) and it quarters. In the limit, \(dA/dr = 2\pi r\).

Act 1 / The habit

One move, used all day

$$\underbrace{dA}_{\text{change out}} \;\approx\; \underbrace{2\pi r}_{\text{rate}} \cdot \underbrace{dr}_{\text{change in}}$$

The rate is the derivative. The leftover \(dr^2\) term is what we throw away.
