Everything in this block

Nudge

\(df \approx f'(x)\,dx\). The error shrinks faster than \(dx\).

Definition

The slope of the tangent: rise over run as the run shrinks to zero.

Rules

Power, sum, product, and the chain rule: local factors multiply.

Partials

\(\partial f/\partial x_i\): nudge one input, freeze the rest.
