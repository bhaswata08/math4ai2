Rules from pictures

Every rule in the table is a shape gaining a sliver of area.

25 min

\(x^2\) is a square

Two strips of area \(x\,dx\), plus a corner \(dx^2\) too small to matter.

\(x^3\) is a cube

Three slabs of volume \(x^2\,dx\). Edges and corner vanish.

The power rule

Pick which of the \(n\) factors gives the \(dx\): \(n\) ways, so \(d(x^n) = n x^{n-1}\,dx\).

\(1/x\): area that must stay 1

Area gained on the side equals area lost on top, so \(d(1/x)/dx = -1/x^2\).

\(\sin\) on the unit circle

Zoomed in, the arc is straight. A similar triangle gives \(d(\sin\theta) = \cos\theta\,d\theta\).
