Block 2.3 / Outcomes

After this block you can

  1. Read a diagonal matrix as a scaling of each axis, and a negative entry as a reflection
  2. Recognise a shear, and say why it keeps area
  3. Test whether a matrix is orthogonal, and say what it preserves
  4. Build a projection \(P = uu^T\) and explain why it cannot be undone
  5. Spot symmetric and positive definite matrices, and know where they appear in AI
  6. Say what an eigenvector is: a direction the matrix does not turn
