How the special matrices fit together

Square matrices \(n\times n\)
Invertible \(\det\neq0\)
Orthogonal\(Q^{-1}=Q^T\), \(\det=\pm1\)
Symmetric \(A=A^T\)
Diagonalscale each axis
Positive semi-definite \(x^TAx\ge0\)
Positive definite\(x^TAx>0\): PSD and invertible
Projections\(P=uu^T\), \(\det=0\)

The identity \(I\) is diagonal, orthogonal and positive definite. Boxes side by side can still overlap: reflections such as \(\mathrm{diag}(-1,1)\) are orthogonal and symmetric. Every positive definite matrix is also invertible.
