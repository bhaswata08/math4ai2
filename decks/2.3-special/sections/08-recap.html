Act 8 / Recap

The family at a glance

MatrixActionKey fact
Diagonalscale each axis; negative flips\(\det=\) product of entries
Shearslant\(\det=1\), area kept
Orthogonalrotate or reflect\(Q^{-1}=Q^T\), lengths kept
Projectiondrop onto a line\(P=uu^T\), \(P^2=P\), \(\det=0\)
Symmetricmirror across diagonal\(A=A^T\)
Positive definitecurves up everywhere\(x^TAx>0\)

An eigenvector is a direction the matrix does not turn: \(Av=\lambda v\).

Act 8 / Practice

By hand

  1. Is \(Q=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}\) orthogonal? Find \(Q^{-1}\) and \(\det Q\). Columns \((0,1)\) and \((1,0)\) are unit and perpendicular, so \(Q^TQ=I\) and \(Q^{-1}=Q^T=Q\). \(\det Q=-1\): a reflection across the line \(y=x\).
  2. Write \(\mathrm{diag}(-2,\,0.5)\) as a reflection times two one-axis scalings.
  3. Build \(P=uu^T\) for \(u=\tfrac15\begin{bmatrix}3\\4\end{bmatrix}\) and check \(P^2=P\).
  4. Is \(\begin{bmatrix}2&3\\3&4\end{bmatrix}\) positive definite?
