Convex optimisation

The class of problems where "found a minimum" means "found the minimum".

20 min

Three starts, one answer

On a convex function every start ends at the same minimum. Without convexity, the start decides.

Act 3 / Definition

A convex problem

$$\min_{\mathbf x}\; f(\mathbf x) \quad \text{s.t.}\quad g_i(\mathbf x) \le 0,\quad A\mathbf x = \mathbf b$$

Requirements

\(f\) and every \(g_i\) convex; equality constraints affine. Then the feasible set is convex.

Guarantee

Every local minimum is global, and solvers find it reliably. Inequalities are handled by the KKT conditions, the generalisation of Lagrange.
