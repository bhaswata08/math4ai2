Act 4 / One training step

Every idea in one line of code

$$\mathbf w \leftarrow \mathbf w - \eta\,\frac1B\sum_{i \in \text{batch}} \nabla_{\mathbf w}\,\ell\big(\sigma(W\mathbf x_i + \mathbf b),\ y_i\big)$$

Days 1–2

\(W\mathbf x_i + \mathbf b\): vectors and an affine map.

Day 3

\(\nabla_{\mathbf w}\): partial derivatives, the Jacobian, the chain rule.

Day 4

Backpropagation, a learning rate, a mini-batch.

Act 4 / Mixed practice

Problems that cross days

  1. Minimise \(\lVert A\mathbf x - \mathbf b\rVert^2\): set the gradient to zero. \(A^{\top}A\,\mathbf x = A^{\top}\mathbf b\), the normal equations
  2. Show that the Hessian of \(\lVert A\mathbf x - \mathbf b\rVert^2\) is \(2A^{\top}A\), and that it is positive semi-definite. What does that say about the problem?
  3. Maximise \(\mathbf w^{\top}\Sigma\mathbf w\) with \(\lVert\mathbf w\rVert = 1\) for \(\Sigma = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}\). Which Day 3 method have you just done?
  4. For \(L(\mathbf w) = \mathbf w^{\top}\Sigma\mathbf w\), what is the largest stable learning rate for gradient descent?
